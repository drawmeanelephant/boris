# Accessibility notes

The Textpattern port keeps the strong editorial identity without making the
page depend on color, hover, a pointer, or a runtime script.

- Each layout has a skip link to `#main-content` (a `main` with
  `tabindex="-1"`), a banner/header, and a contentinfo footer.
- Navigation, breadcrumbs, the table of contents, children, and relations
  remain ordinary links generated by Boris. The navigation disclosure uses
  native `<details>` and `<summary>` so it remains keyboard-operable without
  JavaScript.
- Focus rings are a solid 3px outline with a 3px offset. On paper they are
  crimson (`#8c2323`, 8.82:1 on white; `#e18c82`, 5.56:1 on the dark paper).
  Inside the masthead they switch to gold (`#d6a04a`, 6.03:1 on the light
  masthead) or to the paper colour in dark mode (12.18:1), because gold alone
  measures only 2.34:1 on white paper.
- The mark is decorative where adjacent text already names the site; the
  page-local press-cycle diagram has descriptive alternative text and SVG
  metadata.
- Tables scroll horizontally when needed instead of shrinking into an
  unreadable grid. Code blocks are scrollable and preserve their language
  class from Oliver.
- The desktop two/three-column compositions collapse into a single reading
  order at 860px. Navigation, metadata, children, and the TOC are never
  hidden; they become normal stacked regions.
- `prefers-color-scheme: dark` changes the paper, ink, rule, and code tokens,
  while `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables smooth scrolling and
  transitions.
- Callouts retain a text label (`Note`, `Tip`, `Info`, `Warning`, or `Danger`)
  in addition to their border color. Native disclosure summaries retain a
  visible marker and focus state. Draft and archived status stamps keep the
  status word; relation kinds are printed as words ("depends on").
- `@media print` resets every palette token to black on white, even for
  dark-mode readers, hides the skip link, sidebars, and footer, and keeps
  the article, metadata, tags, and relations in one column.
- `data-boris-search-root` sits on `main`. Sidebars and the masthead live
  outside it; the relations wrapper carries `data-boris-search-exclude`; the
  compiler already excludes `dl.page-metadata` and `ul.page-tags`.

This is a theme-level accessibility pass. It does not claim to replace a
manual screen-reader, keyboard, zoom, or contrast review of the rendered
site.
