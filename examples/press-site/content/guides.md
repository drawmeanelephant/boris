# Guides

These pages are the practical side of the port: how the files fit together,
how the visual system is expressed in CSS, and how to keep authored content
pleasant when the viewport gets small.

## A section with a real graph

The direct child cards below come from Boris's graph rather than a hand-written
list. Read [Getting started](guides/getting-started.md) first, then visit
[Components and content](guides/components.md) for the Markdown surfaces this
theme is meant to showcase.

<Aside kind="note">

The section layout gives `{{children}}` a prominent place because a section
landing page should help a reader choose the next page without scanning the
entire site navigation.

</Aside>

## The house style

Use a strong title, a short opening paragraph, and meaningful subheads. Let
the paper surface carry the reading experience; use crimson for links and
focus, not as a wall of decoration.
