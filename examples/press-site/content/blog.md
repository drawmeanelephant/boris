# Journal

Short reports from the workbench: what the port is preserving, what the
browser should do at the edges, and which details are worth keeping visible.

The post pages share a blog layout selected by the `blog/*` glob. The landing
page is selected by its exact `id:blog` rule, keeping the rule set explicit and
easy to transplant.

<Aside kind="tip">

The journal is content, not a pretend comment system. Any imported comments,
dates, or bylines should remain clearly authored static metadata.

</Aside>
