# Keeping the paper

The first decision was to keep the quiet surface. A modern theme can have a
dark masthead, crimson links, and a sturdy grid without turning every region
into a card.

## What survived the translation

- Serif text for long reading.
- Sans-serif utility labels and metadata.
- Monospace for paths, code, and small archival signals.
- Rules that organize rather than decorate.

The result feels like a publication with a filing system, which is exactly the
useful tension in the old Textpattern references.
