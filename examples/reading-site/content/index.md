# Reading

This sample site exists so the shipped Reading theme can show its
`:lang(...)` rules. The English around these paragraphs is the reviewable
corpus. The short passages are ordinary sentences, not a pretend
publication in another language.

<div class="specimen" lang="ja">
<p>これは英語圏向けの文書テーマではありません。行間を広くし、斜体では強調しません。</p>
</div>

<div class="specimen" lang="zh">
<p>这不是为美式英文文档设计的主题。行距更宽，也不用斜体表示强调。</p>
</div>

</div>
